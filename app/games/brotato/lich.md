# Brotato Lich Guide: Healing Build, Weapons and Stats

> **Quick Answer:** Lich turns healing into extra damage against a random enemy, so HP Regeneration and Life Steal both matter. RUNBACK tested a Medical Gun start on Danger 0 and verified the setup through the Wave 8 shop. The healing loop was useful, but direct weapon damage still mattered; by Wave 8 the loadout had become a four-weapon hybrid rather than an all-Medical-Gun setup.

## Lich Traits and How They Work

Lich starts with a healing-focused kit:

- +10 HP Regeneration
- +10% Life Steal
- healing can trigger damage to a random enemy
- Damage modifications are reduced by 50%

The last line should not be read as "Lich deals 50% less total damage." It means Lich gets reduced benefit from Damage modifications.

The tested character screen also confirms Medical Gun as the starting weapon used for this run.

![Lich selected with Medical Gun on Danger 0](LCH-01.png)

*Lich selected with Medical Gun for the tested Danger 0 run. The current character screen shows +10 HP Regeneration, +10% Life Steal, healing-trigger damage and reduced Damage modifications.*

## How Lich's Healing Damage Works

The basic loop is:

**healing event → passive trigger → damage to a random enemy**

In this test, both HP Regeneration and Life Steal felt relevant.

HP Regeneration provides passive healing over time. Life Steal requires attacking and successful Life Steal healing events. The test does not support ranking one above the other.

The passive itself was noticeable and useful, but its trigger frequency felt **occasional**, not constant.

That leads to the most important conclusion for this build:

**healing adds damage; it does not replace weapons.**

## Why Medical Gun Is a Simple Starting Weapon

Medical Gun is a straightforward starting direction because it matches Lich's healing theme.

It is a ranged Medical + Gun weapon and has strong Life Steal behavior. The Medical weapon class also supports the healing-focused direction.

That makes Medical Gun a useful **starting weapon and healing-engine starter**, not a mandatory final loadout.

The Lich selection screen displays 60% Life Steal on the Medical Gun, but that displayed value should not be treated as universal base weapon data because Lich's own Life Steal bonus is already active on the character screen.

## HP Regen vs Life Steal

This test does not support ranking one above the other.

The player felt that **both mattered**.

HP Regeneration contributes periodic healing. Life Steal contributes healing through attacks.

For Lich, both can support the healing-trigger loop.

The practical question is not "which stat always wins?" but whether the run has enough healing frequency without sacrificing too much offense.

## Lich Stat Priorities

This is a direction, not a rigid tier list.

### Healing core

- HP Regeneration
- Life Steal

Both contributed in this test.

### Survivability

- Max HP
- Armor

Healing only helps if Lich survives long enough to benefit from it.

### Offense

- Attack Speed
- appropriate flat damage for the weapons actually being used
- general Damage when useful

Do not use exact thresholds from this run as targets.

The following are snapshots, not goals:

- 36 HP Regeneration
- 9% Life Steal
- 27 Max HP
- 2 Armor

## Why Weapon Damage Still Matters

This was one of the clearest manual observations.

The healing-trigger passive felt useful.

Direct weapon damage still felt weak.

Those statements can both be true.

The passive should be treated as **extra pressure**, not as a replacement for normal offense.

The test does not prove that 4% Damage or 2 Ranged Damage caused the issue. It only suggests that healing synergy does not remove the need to maintain useful direct weapon output.

## Should Lich Intentionally Take Damage?

Not as a basic rule.

During the test, the player sometimes intentionally took damage to create room for healing and trigger Lich's passive.

That is a real observed tactic from this run.

But it is not evidence that deliberately taking hits is optimal or required.

Because the passive was only occasional, intentionally taking damage should be treated as an optional risk/reward tactic, not the default loop.

## Flexible Weapons After the Opening

The test started with Medical Gun but became more flexible.

By Wave 8, Rail Gun II and Circular Saw III had joined the loadout.

That supports a hybrid approach.

If a useful direct-damage weapon appears, taking it can be more sensible than spending heavily to force another Medical Gun.

The player also felt that more close-range or direct-damage support would have helped after Wave 6.

However, the verified Wave 8 snapshot confirms only one clear melee weapon: Circular Saw III.

So this page does **not** claim that two melee weapons are required after Wave 6.

## What Happened After Wave 8

The manual evidence is verified through the **Wave 8 shop**.

The player later reported that movement and positioning became more important after Wave 8.

The exact final stopping wave was not recorded.

Therefore this guide does not claim:

- Wave 9 reached
- Wave 10 reached
- Danger 0 cleared
- boss tested
- full run completed

The correct conclusion is:

**The tested setup felt reasonably strong through the verified Wave 8 snapshot, but the player reported that positioning became more important after Wave 8.**

## Common Lich Mistakes

### Forcing six Medical Guns

The test did not prove that six Medical Guns are required or even desirable.

### Ignoring direct damage

Healing-trigger damage was useful, but it did not replace weapon output.

### Treating the -50% trait as total damage being halved

It refers to reduced benefit from Damage modifications, not a universal half-damage rule.

### Taking damage on purpose as a mandatory strategy

The player did this sometimes, but the test does not support making it a baseline recommendation.

### Ranking HP Regen over Life Steal — or the reverse

Both felt useful in this run. One short test is not enough to rank them.

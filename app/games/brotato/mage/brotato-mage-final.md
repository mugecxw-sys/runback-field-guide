# Brotato Mage Taser Build and How to Play Mage

> **Quick Answer:** Mage naturally favors Elemental Damage, and Taser is one of Mage's available starting weapons that fits that scaling direction. In RUNBACK's Danger 0 test, a six-Taser Elemental setup reached Wave 9. The damage direction still worked, but with only 23 Max HP and 0 Armor, the run had become noticeably fragile and positioning mattered more.

## Brotato Mage traits

Mage is built around Elemental Damage rather than conventional Melee or Ranged scaling.

The character gets **+25% to Elemental Damage modifications**, starts with **Snake** and **Scared Sausage**, and takes heavy penalties to Melee Damage, Ranged Damage, and Engineering modifications. That makes Elemental weapons the clearest starting point for a straightforward Mage run.

Snake and Scared Sausage also support burn-focused play. Snake helps burning spread to another nearby enemy, while Scared Sausage can add burn to attacks.

Those are source-backed Mage mechanics, not something this RUNBACK test attempted to isolate or measure.

## Tested Mage Taser build

This guide is based on a limited manual test, not a full-game clear.

| Test detail | Result |
|---|---|
| Difficulty | Danger 0 |
| Starting weapon | Taser |
| Reached | Wave 9 |
| Final weapons | 5 × Taser II, 1 × Taser I |
| Elemental Damage | 23 |
| Attack Speed | -7% |
| Max HP | 23 |
| Armor | 0 |

The tested setup reached Wave 9 with five Tier II Tasers and one Tier I Taser.

That is a record of this run, **not a recipe you need to copy exactly**.

The useful pattern is simpler:

Start with Taser, keep adding Tasers when the shop gives you reasonable opportunities, combine duplicates as you build the weapon set, and use Elemental Damage as the main offensive direction.

**This was a partial Danger 0 test. RUNBACK did not complete the run, test Danger 5, test bosses, or prove that six Tasers are Mage's strongest possible setup.**

## Why Taser works with Mage

Taser is both a **Support** and **Elemental** weapon and scales with Elemental Damage.

Its projectiles also slow enemies in an area around the projectile. Higher-tier Tasers gain additional projectiles, giving the weapon more ways to pressure enemies as waves become busier.

Its weapon classes also fit Mage well.

Stacking Elemental-class weapons can contribute additional Elemental Damage, while Support-class stacking can contribute Harvesting.

Mage's starting Snake and Scared Sausage add another Elemental and burn layer to the character.

That does not mean this test proved how much damage came from burning. RUNBACK did not separately measure burn chance, burn DPS, or the percentage of total damage caused by direct Taser hits versus burning effects.

## How to play Mage with Tasers

### Waves 1–3: build the weapon base

Do not stand still and trade hits unnecessarily.

During waves, focus on movement, spacing, and collecting materials while your Taser setup handles nearby enemies.

When the shop offers another useful Taser, adding it helps build toward a fuller weapon set. Elemental Damage upgrades also fit the basic direction immediately.

Do not waste large amounts of money trying to force six identical weapons as quickly as possible.

The goal is to establish a functioning weapon set, not reproduce one exact screenshot.

![Mage beginning the tested Danger 0 Taser run](BRT-MAGE-02.png)

*Mage beginning the tested Danger 0 Taser run in Wave 1.*

### Waves 4–6: keep damage growing, but watch your defenses

Continue taking useful Elemental Damage and improving the weapon set when the run gives you reasonable opportunities.

Matching Tasers can later be combined into higher tiers.

At the same time, start watching your survivability.

This is where a Mage run can become misleading: the early waves may feel comfortable enough that spending everything on damage or economy seems harmless.

That became the main weakness of the RUNBACK test later.

Useful defensive options include:

- Max HP
- Armor
- Dodge
- Speed

You do not need to force one exact defensive stat.

Use what the shop and level-up choices give you, but avoid reaching the later waves with every defensive category neglected.

### Waves 7–9: positioning becomes more important

By Waves 8–9, enemy density in the test had increased enough that movement could no longer be treated as an afterthought.

Keep moving and avoid letting several groups close around Mage at once.

Taser's ability to pressure several enemies and slow targets can help create room, but it does not make Mage durable.

![Mage fighting with multiple Tasers in Wave 9](BRT-MAGE-04.png)

*Wave 9 of the tested run. Enemy pressure had increased enough that movement and spacing were becoming important.*

This test does **not** prove that you should always circle clockwise, stay permanently at the edge of the arena, or follow another fixed movement pattern.

The useful takeaway is simpler:

**A low-defense Mage needs increasingly careful positioning as the screen becomes crowded.**

## Stat priority for Taser Mage

This is not a rigid stat tier list.

### 1. Elemental Damage — core damage direction

Elemental Damage is the main offensive direction for this setup.

Mage improves Elemental Damage modifications, while Taser itself scales with Elemental Damage.

That makes Elemental Damage the most direct offensive stat to build around.

### 2. Survivability — important once pressure increases

The biggest problem in this test was not that the Elemental direction stopped working.

The problem was that Mage became too fragile.

At Wave 8, the recorded stats showed:

- 22 Max HP
- 20 Elemental Damage
- 0 Armor

By the final Wave 9 record, the setup had:

- 23 Max HP
- 23 Elemental Damage
- 0 Armor

That is a useful warning against continuing to invest only in damage.

![Wave 8 stats showing strong Elemental investment but thin defenses](BRT-MAGE-03.png)

*Wave 8, Level 10. Elemental Damage had reached 20, but the build still had only 22 Max HP and 0 Armor.*

Once enemies begin putting real pressure on the run, available Max HP, Armor, Dodge, or Speed can become more valuable than simply taking another offensive upgrade.

There is no verified requirement such as "you need X Armor by Wave Y."

### 3. Attack Speed — useful, but not mandatory

Attack Speed can help weapon output, but this test does not support treating it as the first stat every Mage must stack.

The final recorded Wave 9 setup actually had **-7% Attack Speed** and still reached Wave 9.

That does not mean negative Attack Speed is desirable.

It only shows that this early/mid-game test was not dependent on aggressively stacking Attack Speed first.

### 4. Range and economy — secondary

Range and economy are secondary considerations rather than requirements for this tested setup.

Treat them as situational support rather than fixed priorities.

## What went wrong in the test

The tested build did not appear to have the wrong damage direction.

The clearer problem was that the character had become **too fragile**.

By the final recorded state:

- Elemental Damage: 23
- Max HP: 23
- Armor: 0
- Attack Speed: -7%

Mage could still fight through the wave, but the margin for movement mistakes had become noticeably smaller.

That suggests a practical adjustment for another attempt:

**Once your Elemental damage direction is established, stop treating every defensive upgrade as something you can postpone.**

Take useful Max HP, Armor, Dodge, or movement when the run presents them.

## Mage Taser Build FAQ

### Is Taser a Mage starting weapon?

Yes.

Taser is one of Mage's available starting weapons, and it was the starting weapon used in this RUNBACK test.

### Do I need six Tasers?

No.

Six Tasers were used in the tested setup, but six Tasers are not a mandatory Mage recipe.

The RUNBACK test happened to reach Wave 9 with:

**5 × Taser II + 1 × Taser I**

Treat that as one tested setup, not an exact loadout requirement.

### Should I stack Attack Speed first?

This test does not support making that a rule.

Attack Speed can be useful, but the recorded Wave 9 setup ended at **-7% Attack Speed**.

The stronger conclusion from this run is to keep Elemental Damage as your main offensive direction while making sure survivability does not fall too far behind.

### What should I fix if Mage feels too fragile?

Start accepting useful defensive upgrades instead of continuing to buy only damage.

Depending on what appears, that can include:

- Max HP
- Armor
- Dodge
- Speed

This test does not establish exact stat thresholds.

### Did RUNBACK clear Danger 0 with this build?

No.

The manual test stopped at Wave 9.

It provides evidence for the early/mid-game Taser setup and exposes one important weakness, but it does not prove full-clear performance, Danger 5 performance, or boss performance.

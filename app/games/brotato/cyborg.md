# Brotato Cyborg Guide: Minigun, Engineering and Build Strategy

> **Quick Answer:** Cyborg is a two-phase character. In the first half of a wave, the automatic Minigun and other guns use Ranged Damage. Halfway through the wave, 100% of Ranged Damage is temporarily converted into Engineering at a rate of 1 Ranged Damage = 2 Engineering, so structures and other Engineering-based damage become more important. RUNBACK's Danger 0 test used Wrench with the automatic Minigun and verified the hybrid through Wave 8.

## Cyborg Traits and How the Conversion Works

The current character screen confirms:

- Ranged Damage modifications +200%
- 100% of Ranged Damage is temporarily converted into Engineering halfway through a wave
- 1 Ranged Damage = 2 Engineering
- Engineering modifications -75%
- Melee Damage modifications -100%
- Elemental Damage modifications -100%
- Cyborg automatically carries Minigun

The key word is **temporarily**. Cyborg does not permanently lose Ranged Damage.

The player reported that first-half Minigun damage felt strong, second-half Engineering damage felt strong, and the transition itself was clear.

The build is therefore best understood as:

**guns first half → engineering second half**

It is not a pure Gun build and not a pure Engineering build.


## Early Game: Building Ranged Damage

At the Wave 4 shop, the main test had:

- 4/6 weapons
- automatic Minigun
- 3 × Wrench
- 26 Max HP
- 7% Damage
- 9 Ranged Damage
- 5% Attack Speed
- 1 Armor
- 0 Engineering in the shop

![Wave 4 Cyborg shop snapshot](CYB-02.png)

*Wave 4 shop snapshot from the tested Danger 0 run, with the automatic Minigun and three Wrenches alongside 9 Ranged Damage.*

This shows that a Minigun + Wrench hybrid can form naturally in the early game. It does **not** prove that three Wrenches are a fixed recipe.

Prioritize Ranged Damage when good opportunities appear, keep useful Attack Speed and general Damage, and add Wrenches or other suitable Engineering support when the shop offers them. Do not bankrupt the run forcing one exact weapon setup.

## What Actually Happens at the Mid-Wave Conversion

The clearest mechanic evidence came from a separate Wave 5 capture. It should not be presented as part of the CYB-02 → CYB-04 main-test sequence.

Before conversion:

- Ranged Damage: 12
- Engineering: 0
- Attack Speed: 15%

![Before Cyborg conversion](CYB-03-1.png)

*Separate Wave 5 mechanic capture before the conversion, showing 12 Ranged Damage and 0 Engineering.*

After conversion:

- Ranged Damage: 0
- Engineering: 24
- Attack Speed: 15%

![After Cyborg conversion](CYB-03-2.png)

*The same Wave 5 mechanic capture after the conversion, showing 0 Ranged Damage and 24 Engineering.*

This directly confirms:

**12 Ranged Damage → 24 Engineering**

The conversion is temporary. Ranged Damage returns outside the converted half-wave state.


## Why Engineering Matters in the Second Half

The two halves of the wave play differently.

### First half

The automatic Minigun and other guns make direct use of Ranged Damage. In the test, Minigun damage felt strong.

### Second half

Once Ranged Damage converts into Engineering, the Engineering side becomes more important. Wrench-created structures and other Engineering-based sources can take advantage of the converted stat.

The player felt that Engineering damage was strong in the second half.

The player felt Engineering damage was strong in the second half, but this run did not isolate its share of total damage or determine whether guns stop contributing after conversion.

## Cyborg Stat Priorities

This is a direction, not a threshold list.

### Primary: Ranged Damage

Ranged Damage supports both phases:

- it powers the first-half gun phase directly
- it temporarily becomes Engineering for the second half

However, Ranged Damage upgrade choices did not appear every time in the manual test. So the correct advice is not "only stack Ranged Damage." Prioritize it when reasonable opportunities appear while keeping the overall build functional.

### Useful offense: Attack Speed and general Damage

Attack Speed supports the gun phase, while general Damage can help overall output.

By the Wave 8 shop, the test had 25% Damage, 16% Attack Speed, and 15 Ranged Damage. These are snapshots, not targets.

### Engineering support

Wrench is a simple way to support the second half because it provides structures without requiring heavy raw Engineering investment.

Cyborg has an Engineering modification penalty; this run did not test aggressive investment in normal Engineering.

The stronger concept is:

**build Ranged Damage first, then let the temporary conversion power Engineering sources.**

### Survivability

Useful defensive options include:

- Max HP
- Armor
- Dodge
- Speed as needed

The tested Wave 8 values are descriptive, not required thresholds.


## Should You Force More Miniguns or Wrenches?

No.

The automatic Minigun is guaranteed by Cyborg. During this test, **no additional Minigun appeared in the shop**.

That does not mean extra Miniguns never appear.

Wrench also did not appear frequently enough to justify a six-Wrench recommendation.

The better approach is a flexible hybrid:

- keep the automatic Minigun
- take useful guns when they fit
- add Wrenches or other Engineering support when available
- avoid overspending to force a perfect weapon theme

## Survivability and Life Steal

The Wave 8 test had **0% Life Steal**.

The player felt that some Life Steal could have improved margin for error.

That is a player observation, not a requirement.

Life Steal can be treated as an optional survivability tool if it appears in a reasonable choice, but it is not mandatory.

## Common Cyborg Mistakes

### Building only guns

Cyborg is not just a gun character. The second half changes the value of the build because Ranged Damage temporarily becomes Engineering.

### Building only Engineering

The first half still relies heavily on Ranged Damage and gun output, and Cyborg has an Engineering modification penalty.

### Forcing six Wrenches

The tested run used Wrenches, but their availability did not support a pure Wrench recipe.

### Assuming extra Miniguns are easy to find

No additional Minigun appeared during this test. That is a single-run observation, not a universal rule.

### Forgetting the conversion is temporary

The Wave 8 shop returned to 15 Ranged Damage and 0 Engineering because the wave had ended.

### Ignoring survivability

Danger 0 felt easy through the verified test, but that does not make defense optional.


## What This Test Actually Proved

The Danger 0 manual test supports these conclusions:

- the automatic Minigun provided strong first-half pressure
- Wrench fit naturally into the hybrid
- the mid-wave transition was clearly visible
- 12 Ranged Damage converted into 24 Engineering
- the player felt second-half Engineering damage was strong
- the Wave 8 shop returned to normal Ranged Damage display
- no additional Minigun appeared during this test
- Wrenches were not frequent enough to justify forcing six
- the build felt easy on Danger 0 through the verified range

The test does **not** prove:

- a full Danger 0 clear
- Danger 5 viability
- a best or meta build
- exact gun-versus-Engineering DPS contribution
- that extra Miniguns are impossible to find
- that six Wrenches are optimal
- that Life Steal is required

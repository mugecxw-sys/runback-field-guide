# Wanderburg Wiki & Reference

A practical overview of Wanderburg's current Early Access systems and where to find details for each one.

> **Current coverage:** Early Access 0.9.14

## Wanderburg at a Glance

Wanderburg is a single-player action roguelike built around a moving castle. Drive your fortress across the map, consume smaller targets, collect resources, choose upgrades and fight increasingly dangerous enemy fortresses.

A run brings together your vehicle, its Module slots, your Captain, Starter Artifacts, Crew and the upgrades offered as you progress. The options you see can vary between runs, so adapt your setup to the choices available.

## Core Systems

### Vehicles

Vehicles determine your Module slots and affect how your fortress moves. Compare the current slot layouts and characteristics in the [Wanderburg Vehicles Wiki](/games/wanderburg/wiki/vehicles).

### Modules

Modules attach to your vehicle and provide attacks or utility. The [Wanderburg Modules Wiki](/games/wanderburg/wiki/modules) lists all 20 Modules shown in the current client, including their slots, effects and available base values.

### Captains

Captains pair a positive effect with a drawback, changing the trade-offs of a run. See all 14 current Captains and their effects in the [Wanderburg Captains Wiki](/games/wanderburg/wiki/captains).

### Artifacts

Starter Artifacts add effects to a run. The [Wanderburg Artifacts Wiki](/games/wanderburg/wiki/artifacts) covers the 21 choices in the current Starter Artifact selection and their displayed effects.

### Crew

Crew provide support during a run, including attacks, repairs and Gold generation. See the six current Crew entries in the [Wanderburg Crew Wiki](/games/wanderburg/wiki/crew).

## Progression and Rerolls

Permanent unlock progress grants separate rerolls for Module and Artifact choices:

- Every **6 unlocked Modules** grants **+1 New Module Reroll**.
- Every **6 unlocked Artifacts** grants **+1 Artifact Reroll**.
- You start with **2 Artifact rerolls**.

## Bosses and Overtime

Bosses are major progression encounters and drop at least **50 Silver**. During boss encounters, the camera zooms out slightly. Some larger enemies do not take percentage-based damage from boss explosions.

Overtime changes the encounters after the main progression. Enemy stun resistance and the Grassland, Desert and Darkforest Overtime encounters have been adjusted; use the current in-game behavior when choosing how to approach them.

# Wanderburg Builds Guide (0.9.14): What to Build Around Now

Wanderburg is still changing quickly in Early Access, so there is an important distinction between a useful build direction and a proven universal “best build.”

As of Hotfix 0.9.14, there is not enough current evidence to call one loadout the best setup for every vehicle, biome, boss, or progression level.

Instead, this guide focuses on mechanics that are confirmed in the current Early Access patch history and explains what they mean when you are deciding what to build around.

> **Current coverage:** Early Access 0.9.14
> **Last reviewed:** September 22, 2026

---

## Is There a Best Wanderburg Build?

Not one that RUNBACK can currently support as a universal answer.

Wanderburg builds depend on what you have unlocked, which modules appear during the run, your vehicle slots, your Captain, and the upgrades offered to you.

The balance is also moving quickly.

Since launch, official hotfixes have already changed:

- Electric Mage
- Side Arms
- Side Ballista
- Front Barracks
- Back Turret
- allied units
- several Captains
- reroll availability
- Overtime balance

That makes launch-day tier lists and fixed “meta” loadouts especially easy to date.

A better approach in the current build is to choose one mechanic that solves a problem in your run and build around that before adding support pieces.

## Current Build Directions Worth Testing

## 1. Electric Mage: Multi-Target Magic

Electric Mage is one of the clearest modules to revisit after Hotfix 0.9.14.

The update increased its base auto-attack target count from:

**5 → 7**

Its upgrades can also grant more targets per attack.

Hotfix 0.9.14 additionally improved the reliability of its **Roaming Storm** effect.

Earlier, Hotfix 0.9.10 fixed Electric Mage so that it no longer stunned allied units.

### What this means

Electric Mage now deserves another look when your run needs attacks that can affect several targets instead of concentrating everything on one enemy.

The patch itself does **not** prove that Electric Mage is now the strongest module in Wanderburg.

What it does prove is that older build advice based on the pre-0.9.14 version is no longer a reliable baseline.

### Build around it when

Consider developing the run further around Electric Mage when:

- its automatic attack is contributing meaningful damage
- additional targets solve the pressure you are facing
- its active effect is useful in the encounters you are actually getting
- your remaining slots can cover weaknesses rather than duplicating the same job

Do not force the build simply because Electric Mage was buffed.

---

## 2. Side Arms: Automatic Pressure

Side Arms has now received meaningful improvements across two patches.

Hotfix 0.9.10 increased rarity-based damage for:

- the active ability
- automatic attacks

Hotfix 0.9.13 then improved its base automatic attack interval from:

**3.0 seconds → 2.5 seconds**

### What this means

Side Arms is worth reevaluating when you need damage that continues without depending entirely on repeated active inputs.

The 2.5-second interval is a confirmed current change, but it does not establish a complete best-in-slot ranking.

When Side Arms appears, compare its current offered rarity and effects with what your existing build is already doing.

### Build around it when

Side Arms becomes more interesting when:

- passive damage is currently a weakness
- the offered rarity meaningfully improves the module
- you can keep enemies in useful firing positions
- your other equipment already handles survival or control

---

## 3. Side Ballista: Active-Focused Offense

Side Ballista is a good example of why you should separate a module's automatic attack from its active ability.

Hotfix 0.9.9:

- **nerfed its auto attack**
- **buffed its active ability**

That means “Side Ballista was buffed” is incomplete advice.

One part became weaker while another became stronger.

### What this means

If you pick Side Ballista, evaluate whether you are actually using its active ability effectively.

A player who rarely activates it is not taking advantage of the part that received the improvement.

### Build around it when

It is more attractive when:

- you are comfortable positioning the correct side of the castle toward a target
- you use active abilities deliberately
- the active attack solves an immediate damage problem
- your Captain does not make the required ability timing impractical

This is an active-oriented direction, not proof of a universal Ballista meta.

---

## 4. Front Barracks: Allied-Unit Direction

Front Barracks has also received several relevant changes.

Hotfix 0.9.9:

- fixed diminishing-damage upgrades for Front Barracks
- buffed its active ability
- made allied melee units move faster toward targets

Hotfix 0.9.14 later made allied units immune to:

- fire
- stun
- knockback

The developers noted that allied units were already invincible; the new immunities make their behavior more consistent.

### What this means

Older impressions of allied-unit builds can be misleading because the supporting mechanics have already changed.

Front Barracks is therefore worth reevaluating if you want a run built around allied units and active deployment.

### Build around it when

Consider continuing the direction when:

- allied units are consistently reaching useful targets
- Front Barracks' active ability is contributing to encounters
- your movement does not constantly leave your units behind
- another module can cover situations your allies struggle to reach

The patch changes make the direction more interesting to test.

They do not prove that allied-unit builds guarantee safe boss clears.

---

## 5. Force Mage: Projectile Control

Force Mage fills a different role from pure damage modules.

Hotfix 0.9.9 added a clear mechanic:

**Force Mage's active ability cancels incoming projectiles.**

That makes it useful to evaluate when projectiles are the specific reason your run is becoming difficult.

### What this means

Force Mage should be treated as a response to a problem, not automatically as a damage upgrade.

If incoming projectiles are not the threat ending your runs, another module may provide more value.

The official update confirms projectile cancellation.

It does not say that the ability blocks:

- melee attacks
- collisions
- every area effect
- all forms of damage

### Build around it when

Consider Force Mage when:

- projectile pressure is forcing you out of good positions
- you can time the active ability reliably
- your other modules already provide enough offense

This is a control and survival tool, not evidence of a universal best build.

---

## What About Back Turret?

Be careful with older attack-speed advice.

Hotfix 0.9.10 reduced the **maximum attack-speed boost** of Back Turret.

The official patch notes do not provide a complete new damage or attack-speed table.

That means old recommendations built around extreme Back Turret attack-speed stacking should not automatically be carried into the current version.

Use the values shown in your current client when deciding whether further investment is worthwhile.

## Captains Matter to the Build

Several Captains were rebalanced in Hotfix 0.9.10.

### PatchyThePirate

Enemy Cannon bonus damage was reduced from:

**50% → 25%**

### Kapitalstus Maximus

Gold penalty was reduced from:

**-30% → -15%**

### Huntress

Extra ability cooldown time was reduced from:

**50% → 40%**

### Tankbert

Nitro regeneration penalty was reduced from:

**50% slower → 30% slower**

### Duelist

Extra damage taken while boosting was reduced from:

**50% → 20%**

These changes matter because a Captain's downside can affect whether a build is comfortable to execute.

They do not, by themselves, prove that any of these Captains is the best partner for a specific module.

Choose the Captain after deciding what your build actually needs.

## Use Rerolls Instead of Forcing a Bad Build

Wanderburg's progression now gives additional rerolls as you unlock more equipment.

For every:

**6 unlocked Modules**

you receive:

**+1 New Module Reroll**

For every:

**6 unlocked Artifacts**

you receive:

**+1 Artifact Reroll**

Hotfix 0.9.14 also added a **second Artifact reroll from the start**.

This makes it easier to search for something useful, but it still does not guarantee that one exact multi-module loadout will appear every run.

A practical rule is:

**Build around what is working, then use rerolls to improve the direction rather than trying to reproduce a rigid screenshot-perfect loadout.**

---

## Use Damage Numbers to Test Your Build

Hotfix 0.9.13 added optional damage numbers.

You can enable them in the game settings.

This is useful when comparing modules because visual effects alone can be misleading.

When deciding whether an upgrade is actually helping, watch:

- whether targets die faster
- whether automatic attacks are contributing regularly
- whether your active ability is solving the intended problem
- whether positioning required by the module is realistic
- whether the build still has a safe way to disengage

A build that looks spectacular is not automatically the build doing the most useful work.

## A Simple Build Decision Order

When a new module or upgrade appears, use this order:

### 1. Identify the problem

Are you lacking:

- automatic damage
- burst damage
- multi-target pressure
- projectile control
- allied-unit pressure
- movement or survival?

### 2. Choose one primary direction

Do not invest equally into every mechanic.

Pick the part of the run that is already working and strengthen it.

### 3. Separate automatic and active effects

This matters especially for modules such as Side Ballista, where the two parts have received different balance changes.

### 4. Check your Captain's drawback

A strong module can still feel awkward if the Captain changes the resource or cooldown that the build depends on.

### 5. Add support for the weakness

Once the core direction works, use remaining choices to solve what still ends the run.

## What RUNBACK Is Not Calling “Meta” Yet

For Early Access 0.9.14, RUNBACK is not currently claiming:

- one universal best build
- one best Captain
- guaranteed boss-killing loadouts
- guaranteed no-hit setups
- permanent module tier rankings
- leaderboard-winning combinations

Those claims require stronger current-version gameplay evidence than patch notes alone provide.

Instead, the directions above are based on confirmed current mechanics and balance changes.

Specific complete builds can then be tested separately without turning one successful run into a universal rule.

---

## Current Patch Takeaway

If you played Wanderburg before Hotfix 0.9.14, **Electric Mage is the first offensive module worth reevaluating** because its automatic attack received a direct multi-target buff.

**Side Arms** is also worth another look after its faster base auto attack in 0.9.13.

For active-oriented play, **Side Ballista** and **Front Barracks** have documented active-ability improvements from earlier hotfixes.

When incoming projectiles are the problem, **Force Mage** has a clearly documented defensive role.

Use these as build directions rather than fixed recipes.

Wanderburg is still in Early Access, and the strongest practical combinations can change as Randwerk continues balancing the game.

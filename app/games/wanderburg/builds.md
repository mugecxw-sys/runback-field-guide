# Wanderburg Builds Guide: Modules, Captains & Build Directions (0.9.14)

Use these five directions to shape a run around the Modules and Captain effects currently available. They are adaptable approaches, not fixed loadouts.

> **Current coverage:** Early Access 0.9.14

## Short Answer

Start with the problem your run needs to solve: reaching several enemies, adding automatic attacks, making use of an active attack, deploying allied units, or responding to incoming projectiles. Choose from the Modules actually offered, then use later choices to cover what is still missing.

## How a Wanderburg Build Comes Together

Modules provide attacks and utility attached to your vehicle. Captains add a benefit and drawback, while Artifact choices and upgrades affect how the setup develops. Available choices vary between runs, so keep useful parts of your current setup rather than forcing a fixed recipe.

For current details, see the [Wanderburg Modules Wiki](/games/wanderburg/wiki/modules), [Wanderburg Captains Wiki](/games/wanderburg/wiki/captains), and [Wanderburg Artifacts Wiki](/games/wanderburg/wiki/artifacts).

## Build Directions

### Multi-target attacks

**Core idea:** Reach several enemies with the same attack.

**Module:** Lightning Mage has **7 base automatic attack targets**. Its upgrades can add more targets per attack.

**Captain to consider:** Huntress has **+50% auto attack speed** and **+40% ability cooldown time**. Weigh the attack-speed bonus against longer ability cooldowns in a run that uses both.

### Automatic attacks

**Core idea:** Keep an attack contributing while you drive and handle other actions.

**Module:** Arms has a **2.5-second automatic attack interval**, alongside its active ability.

**What to consider:** Compare the Arms rarity and upgrades actually offered with the rest of your current setup.

### Active side attacks

**Core idea:** Make deliberate use of an active attack as well as the automatic behavior.

**Module:** Side Ballista has both automatic arrow volleys and a large active Ballista attack. Consider how the two attack modes fit your current run.

### Allied-unit direction

**Core idea:** Use allied units as part of your run's attack pattern.

**Module:** Front Barracks spawns Biker Knights. Allied units are immune to fire, stun and knockback.

**Captain to consider:** Empress spawns **100% more friendly units** and has **-30% speed**. Account for both effects when choosing how to move and fight.

### Projectile control

**Core idea:** Respond to incoming projectiles when they are the immediate threat.

**Module:** Force Mage's active ability cancels incoming projectiles. Use it for projectile pressure; it does not replace answers to other threats.

## How to Adapt When the Run Does Not Offer Your First Choice

- Identify what is currently ending the run: target coverage, automatic pressure, active attacks, allied-unit pressure or incoming projectiles.
- Choose among the Modules actually offered rather than forcing a fixed multi-Module recipe.
- Keep the useful parts of your current setup and use later choices to address its clearest gap.
- Consider both the benefit and drawback shown for your Captain.
- Use the in-game damage numbers to compare how much different attacks contribute.

# Wanderburg Builds Guide: Modules, Captains & Build Directions (0.9.14)

Use the directions below to shape a run around current Module effects and confirmed patch changes. They are practical starting points, not ranked full-run builds.

> **Current coverage:** Early Access 0.9.14

## Short Answer

Start with the problem your run needs to solve: reaching several enemies, adding automatic attacks, using an active attack, deploying allied units, or stopping incoming projectiles. Choose a Module that addresses that problem, then use later choices to cover what is still missing.

## How a Wanderburg Build Comes Together

Modules provide the attacks and utility attached to your vehicle. Captains add a visible benefit and drawback, while Artifact choices and the upgrades offered during a run affect how the setup develops. The available choices change between runs, so use these directions as adaptable priorities rather than fixed loadouts.

For current names, effects and patch changes, see the [Wanderburg Modules Wiki](/games/wanderburg/wiki/modules), [Wanderburg Captains Wiki](/games/wanderburg/wiki/captains) and [Wanderburg Artifacts Wiki](/games/wanderburg/wiki/artifacts).

## Build Directions

### Multi-target attacks

**Core idea:** Add attacks that can reach several enemies.

**Module:** Lightning Mage.

Hotfix 0.9.14 raised Lightning Mage's base automatic-attack target count from **5 to 7**. The update also says its upgrades can add more targets per attack. When those upgrades appear, they directly extend the multi-target role. The official notes call this Module Electric Mage; the current client name is Lightning Mage.

**Captain to compare:** Huntress's current tooltip lists **+50% auto attack speed** and **+40% ability cooldown time**, which means a longer cooldown. Weigh both effects against how much your run relies on automatic attacks and active abilities.

### Automatic attacks

**Core idea:** Keep an automatic attack contributing while you drive and handle other actions.

**Module:** Arms.

Hotfix 0.9.13 changed the base automatic-attack interval for the Side Arm Module from **3.0 seconds to 2.5 seconds**. The current client calls it Arms. Hotfix 0.9.10 also increased rarity-based damage for its active and automatic attacks.

**What to prioritize:** Compare the offered Arms rarity and upgrades with the rest of your current setup.

### Active side attacks

**Core idea:** Make deliberate use of an active attack rather than judging a Module by its automatic behavior alone.

**Module:** Side Ballista.

Hotfix 0.9.9 changed its two attack modes in opposite directions: the automatic attack was nerfed and the active ability was buffed. If you choose Side Ballista, consider how often you will use its active ability and assess the two modes separately.

### Allied-unit direction

**Core idea:** Use a Module whose visible role is to deploy allied units.

**Module:** Front Barracks spawns Biker Knights. Hotfix 0.9.14 made allied units immune to fire, stun and knockback. Earlier updates also changed Front Barracks' active ability and allied melee-unit movement.

**Captain to compare:** Empress's current tooltip shows **100% more friendly units** and **-30% speed**. Consider both effects separately alongside Front Barracks' allied-unit role.

### Projectile control

**Core idea:** Add an active response when incoming projectiles are the immediate problem.

**Module:** Force Mage's active ability cancels incoming projectiles, as stated in Hotfix 0.9.9. Use it when projectile pressure is the problem, and use other choices to address threats it does not cover.

## How to Adapt When the Run Does Not Offer Your First Choice

- Identify what is currently ending the run: target coverage, automatic pressure, active damage, allied-unit pressure or projectiles.
- Choose among the Modules that are actually offered; do not spend rerolls trying to force a fixed multi-Module recipe.
- Keep the useful part of the current setup and use later choices to address its clearest gap.
- Check both the benefit and drawback shown for your Captain.
- Use the in-game damage-number option, added in Hotfix 0.9.13, when you want to compare damage during your own run.

## Patch 0.9.14 Build Changes That Matter

- **Lightning Mage:** base automatic-attack targets increased from **5 to 7**; upgrades can add more targets.
- **Allied units:** immune to fire, stun and knockback.
- **Artifacts:** a second Artifact reroll is available from the start; Repair Wrench changed from **25% healing to a flat 25 HP heal**.
- **Boss encounters:** the camera zooms out slightly, and some larger enemies no longer take percentage-based damage from boss explosions.

Other confirmed changes that still affect build choices include the Arms automatic-attack interval change in 0.9.13, and the Side Ballista, Front Barracks and Force Mage changes in 0.9.9. See the [Modules Wiki](/games/wanderburg/wiki/modules) and [Artifacts Wiki](/games/wanderburg/wiki/artifacts) for the details.
